## What each one is for

| | What it does | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Imodium** (loperamide 2mg) | Slows the gut — stops the symptoms | You need to function: a train day, a flight, a tour day |
| **Kalbeten** (bismuth subsalicylate 262mg) | Milder — calms the gut, some antibacterial effect | Mild upset, when you are not in a hurry |
| **Azithromycin** (250mg, Azenil / Zeto) | Antibiotic — treats the cause if it is bacterial | Moderate to severe, or not improving |

## When to use what

### 🟢 Mild — a few loose stools, you can carry on
- **Fluids first**
- Then **either** Kalbeten **or** Imodium if you need to — **not both**

### 🟠 Moderate — it is disrupting your day
- **Imodium**
- Not settling within 24 hours → **start azithromycin as well**
- Imodium + azithromycin together is a standard, safe combination

### 🔴 Severe — fever, or blood in the stool
- **Azithromycin**
- ❌ **NO Imodium** — it traps the infection in
- **Tell your Intrepid leader and get seen** · emergency line **+86 17200311621**
- Travel insurance: **PassportCard** — policy and 24hr number are printed in your documents

- ❌ **Never take Imodium and Kalbeten together.** Pick one.
- ❌ **Never take Kalbeten with Celebrex, ibuprofen or any other anti-inflammatory.** Kalbeten is a salicylate, a relative of aspirin — together they raise the risk of stomach bleeding.
- ⚠ **Kalbeten + SSRI** — SSRIs also raise bleeding risk with aspirin-type drugs, so **Imodium is the first choice**. Celebrex also slightly raises bleeding risk with an SSRI — take it with food.
- ⚠ **Azithromycin and magnesium** — take them **at least 2 hours apart.**
- ⚠ **Thyroid pill** — keep **at least 4 hours** away from iron, magnesium **and Kalbeten.**
- ⏱ **Imodium: stop after 48 hours.** Still needing it after that means azithromycin, and probably a doctor.
- 💩 **Kalbeten turns the tongue and stool black.** Harmless — but alarming if you do not know.
