# Land routes if Ben Gurion closes — notes (not on the site)

Saved 5 Oct 2026. Check crossings, airspace and flights on the day — all of this can change fast.

## Which airport for Asia
**Amman (Queen Alia) is best.**
- **Bangkok:** Royal Jordanian direct, ~2 a week, 8–10 hrs — check which days. One stop via Dubai / Doha / Abu Dhabi / Istanbul most days
- **Hong Kong:** one stop via the Gulf or Istanbul, many a day
- Most flights to rebook onto if one is cancelled

**Sharm el-Sheikh:** holiday airport, one-stop only, fewer options. **Taba:** very few flights.

**Trade-off:** in a war with Iran, Jordanian airspace can close (it did briefly in June 2025). Sharm is further from that — Taba → Sharm was the main route out last time.

**Plan:** aim for Amman; on the day check Jordanian airspace is open and Amman flights are departing. If not → Taba → Sharm.

## Getting to the border
Leave Israel on the **Israeli** passport; **UK** passport after the border.

| | ⭐ Sheikh Hussein (north) | Yitzhak Rabin (Eilat) | Taba (Egypt) |
|---|---|---|---|
| From the centre | ~1½–2 hrs car/taxi, near Beit She'an | ~4½–5 hrs bus to Eilat + 10 min taxi | ~4½–5 hrs to Eilat + 10 min taxi |
| Hours | Limited — check | Sun–Thu 06:30–20:00, Fri–Sat 08:00–20:00 | 24 hrs |
| After | Taxi ~1½–2 hrs to Amman airport | Aqaba (few flights) or ~4 hrs to Amman | Taba airport, or ~4 hrs to Sharm |
| Visa | UK: on arrival, ~JOD 40 | Free (Aqaba zone) | Sinai entry; ~USD 25–30 cash |

- Israeli exit fee ~₪100–110 at land crossings · carry cash
- Israel's NSC warns against Sinai and Jordan — go straight to the airport, don't stay
- June 2025: Israelis were told not to use the land crossings; thousands still left via Taba
- Israel Airports Authority site lists which crossings are open
